# SMBC

During my internship at SMBC Nikkou Securities Inc., a subsidiary of Sumitomo Mitsui Banking Corporation (SMBC), I gained hands-on experience in finance, investment products, and the operational workings of major financial institutions in Japan. The internship was conducted entirely in Japanese, which allowed me to apply and improve my language skills in professional, real-world settings. I learned how banks and securities firms contribute to the larger goal of transitioning Japan from a cash-centric society to card-based and digital payments. Day-to-day, I studied different financial instruments, including stocks, ETFs, options, and mutual funds, analyzing their risk profiles, expected returns, and market behavior.

Projects & Learning Experiences
Throughout my internship, I was exposed to a wide range of practical and observational experiences, all conducted in Japanese:
	•	Investment Product Analysis – Learned to differentiate risk levels among various securities, ETFs, and mutual funds, analyzing trends and discussing findings entirely in Japanese with colleagues.
	•	Cashless Payment Initiatives – Observed SMBC’s strategies for promoting card and digital payments in Japan, participating in discussions and presentations in Japanese.
	•	Site Visits & Field Learning – Visited key financial and historical institutions in Osaka, engaging with staff and interpreting materials in Japanese:
	•	Osaka Stock Exchange – Observed market operations and trading floor dynamics.
	•	Bank of Japan, Osaka Branch – Learned about central banking functions and monetary policy.
	•	Osaka Mint – Explored currency production processes and secure money supply management.
	•	Sakishima Cosmo Tower Observatory – Observed Osaka Bay and the Osaka Expo 2025 “Grand Ring” construction, linking infrastructure investment to financial strategy.
	•	Panasonic Museum – Studied corporate history and technological innovation, connecting real-world corporate decisions to finance.

Collaboration & Professional Development
Working entirely in Japanese, I collaborated closely with professionals and other interns, developing strong cross-cultural communication skills. I learned to ask insightful questions, present observations, and participate in team discussions, all while navigating nuanced Japanese business etiquette.

Problem-Solving & Analytical Growth
I enhanced my analytical skills by evaluating risk and return across different investment products. For example, I assessed the volatility of individual stocks versus ETFs and explored how options and mutual funds can diversify or hedge portfolios. Discussing these topics in Japanese strengthened both my technical and linguistic proficiency.

Soft Skills & Work Ethic
I approached the internship with curiosity, detail-orientation, and a proactive mindset. Conducting all tasks in Japanese encouraged me to clarify understanding, take initiative, and adapt quickly to professional and cultural expectations.

Lessons Learned & Growth
This internship deepened my understanding of finance in a Japanese context, improved my Japanese language skills in professional environments, and gave me practical experience in evaluating financial products and observing economic infrastructure. I left confident in my ability to contribute to finance and investment projects while navigating Japanese-language business contexts.
